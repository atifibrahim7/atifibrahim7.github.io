DOCUMENT CHUNKING / RAG INFRASTRUCTURE03
MEASURED BOUNDARIES, NOT COUNTED ONES.
APPLIED AI

ChunkyMonk

A document-chunking engine that decides where to cut text by measuring meaning, not by counting characters, so a qualifier never gets silently separated from the rule it modifies.

PythonFastAPIsentence-transformersVue 3
Engineering details +
Every segment gets a velocity score for how much its meaning shifted. Boundaries land only in the low-shift valleys between load-bearing pivots, and a cut too close to a pivot gets pushed back rather than allowed through.Read the case study ↗
RETRIEVAL ENGINE04
RETRIEVAL THAT CAN SAY NO.
APPLIED AI

Black RAG

A retrieval engine that re-ranks on more than similarity, and returns a typed refusal instead of a confident guess when nothing in the corpus actually answers the question.

PythonFastAPIQdrant / WeaviateVue 3
Engineering details +
Three inspectable stages, Retrieve, Resonate, Marshal, over-fetch and re-rank candidates, apply an admission gate, and escalate before ever conceding a refusal. Every decision is written to a hash-chained audit ledger.Read the case study ↗
RETRIEVAL RETROFIT05
BETTER RETRIEVAL. SAME STORE.
APPLIED AI

BlackPirate RAG

Retrofits advanced retrieval onto a vector store you already built, Qdrant, Weaviate, or pgvector, without rechunking or re-embedding, even when the original chunk text is gone.

PythonFastAPIQdrant / Weaviate / pgvectorVue 3
Engineering details +
Classifies every chunk from full text down to embedding-only, reconstructs what it can from embedding geometry, and stamps every inferred value with a confidence tag it calibrates against ground truth it deliberately hides from itself.Read the case study ↗
