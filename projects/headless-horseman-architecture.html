ARCHITECTURE HEADLESS HORSEMAN

How a call flows

One request, followed from the caller to the bill. The green path is what happens on every single call.

Step by step.

  1. Gateway. The request arrives with a key. Horseman checks the key is real and allowed to do this. If a client retries the same request, it is recognised and not counted twice.
  2. Decide. The call is checked against the customer's plan: how fast they may call, how much is left this month, how many calls can run at once, whether they have credit, and what this caller is allowed to touch. For agents, each sub-agent gets equal or fewer permissions than the one that started it. The answer is yes, or no with a reason and a retry time.
  3. Record. Every decision, yes or no, becomes one signed event in a log that can only grow. Batches are sealed with Merkle checkpoints, so changing any old event would break the seal.
  4. Compute. Usage, credit balances, invoices and receipts are not stored separately. They are calculated from the log, so they always match it.
  5. Collect and audit. Stripe handles subscriptions and checkout, agents can pay per call, and a standalone verifier can check the whole log without trusting the server.
