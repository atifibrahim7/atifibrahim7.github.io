DOCUMENT CHUNKING / RAG INFRASTRUCTURE PRODUCTION SYSTEM

ChunkyMonk

Chunk boundaries you can defend.

A document-chunking engine that decides where to cut text by measuring meaning, not by counting characters.

Cutting by counting
is the blind spot.

Fixed-size windows, sentence counts, and embedding-distance thresholds all place chunk boundaries by position, not meaning. The cut lands wherever the counter runs out.

In practice, a boundary can land in the middle of a sentence's logic. A qualifier like "except where the operator has already exceeded the weekly limit" gets separated from the rule it modifies, and the retrieved chunk downstream says the opposite of what the source document actually says. Nothing in the pipeline notices, because nothing was measuring meaning in the first place.

Profiling the document
before it cuts it.

Every segment gets a velocity score: a measure of how much its meaning shifted relative to the segment before it, computed from embedding distance, a language model's internal hidden-state shift, or token-level perplexity, depending on the backend in use. The placement logic on top stays the same regardless of which one is active.

High-velocity points are semantic pivots, the load-bearing ideas where a document's meaning is doing real work. Low-velocity points are connective tissue: valleys. Chunk boundaries are placed only in those valleys.

The core mechanism is what happens when a boundary wants to land near a pivot. Every pivot carries a protection radius, and no cut is allowed to fall inside it; a candidate boundary is pushed back into the nearest valley instead. When a hard length ceiling forces a cut anyway, that gets logged as a forced boundary rather than treated silently as a normal one.

A thermal and load controller adjusts the chunk-size budget under system load, widening or tightening it, without changing where boundaries are allowed to land. Every ingestion decision is recorded for audit.

What makes it
different.

Boundaries come from a measured signal, not a token or character count.

Pivot protection: a candidate cut can be actively refused and relocated if it is too close to a load-bearing idea.

Three interchangeable velocity backends, embedding distance, model hidden-state, or perplexity: the same placement logic works across model types.

Adaptive chunk-size budget under system load, without abandoning the underlying algorithm.

Every chunking decision is hash-logged for audit.

Where it stands.

ChunkyMonk is a production system, actively used as the ingestion layer feeding downstream retrieval.

No independent benchmark numbers are published here on purpose. The severance and hallucination figures associated with this approach come from the design's own validation run, not a separately audited harness, so they are left out rather than overstated.
